Why 5 agents, not 1?
Reliability. Each agent has one clear responsibility, so failures are isolated and output is verifiable at every stage.
Why Airtable as the central DB?
Queryable, visual, and easy to verify by eye — I can audit any step of the pipeline without reading code.
Why consulting firms as sources?
McKinsey, PwC, KPMG and Deloitte data is validated, quantified and credible — pain backed by numbers, not anecdote.
Why the Paul Graham framework?
Brutal honesty over false positives. Better to kill a weak idea early than chase a market that isn't really there.
05 Next Steps

From Data to Action

Ranked opportunities are only useful if they survive scrutiny. Every candidate runs through a Paul Graham–style evaluation before it earns a place in the weekly report.

The Paul Graham Evaluation

  • 1Is the problem real? Pressure-test the pain — frequent and painful, or imagined?
  • 2Who feels it? Validate exactly who suffers and how badly, today.
  • 3Who's competing? Map incumbents and ask why anyone would switch.
  • 4First 10 customers? Name them concretely — or admit you can't.
  • 52-week MVP? Define the smallest test of the riskiest assumption.
